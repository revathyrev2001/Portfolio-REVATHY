About me
RR

Hi, I'm Revathy.

I'm a GTM Operations Associate at AI Intime, part of the founding team and reporting directly to the founder, and a Sales and Marketing Associate at its parent company, Vegam Solutions, in Bengaluru. We sell manufacturing software and industrial AI to chemical, batch and discrete manufacturers, mostly in the US.

I started out building lead lists and running outbound campaigns by hand. Then I got curious about why so much of it was manual, and started building the tools that do it instead: buying-signal engines, account research, lead cleaning, CRM data operations and n8n automation. Today eight of those tools are live on our internal tools server, and you can see them below.

I like problems that sit between people, data and process, and I care about tools that are cheap to run, honest about what they know, and actually used by the sales team.
